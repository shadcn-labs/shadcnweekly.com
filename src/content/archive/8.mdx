## Theme of the Week

This week’s theme spotlights motion‑first and animated component libraries added to the official shadcn registry.

- **[@illos](https://illos.dev)** Animated SVG illustrations for product sites and apps in four styles (Shader, HUD, Isometric, Interface), available in free and pro versions.
- **[@jyotirmoydas](https://jyotimoydas.in/components)** A collection of iOS‑inspired liquid glass components with fluid animations and refractions.
- **[@prequelui](https://prequelui.terravidhal.me)** Animated, open‑source blocks for shadcn/ui featuring charts, metrics, tables, and feeds with purposeful motion, reduced‑motion support, and light/dark themes.
